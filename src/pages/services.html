Cooling

AC repair

The most common call. The system runs but blows warm air, the outdoor unit hums and will not start, ice forms on the copper lines, or water shows up where it should not. A good technician measures before replacing parts, names what failed and prices the fix before starting.

Emergency AC repair

The same work after hours, when heat is a health risk. That page covers who is most at risk in a Dallas heat wave, what to do while you wait, cooling centers, and how after-hours charges usually work.

AC installation and replacement

When repair no longer makes sense. Sizing with a load calculation, efficiency ratings, the newer refrigerants, permits and what a complete written quote should list.

Furnace and heating repair
