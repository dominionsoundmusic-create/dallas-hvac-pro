WHAT THE BUSINESS ACTUALLY IS (read this twice):
Dallas Air & Heating is a FREE PHONE LINE and website, NOT an HVAC company. It has no technicians,
trucks, vans, equipment, license or certification, and never repairs, installs, maintains, quotes or
schedules anything itself. When someone calls (469) 649-7066:
- An automated assistant answers, any hour.
- It asks for the caller's name, callback number, city or ZIP and what the system is doing.
- AC and heating calls are transferred to an independent, locally owned heating and air conditioning
  company. That company hears a short summary before it picks up.
- If that company cannot answer, the caller's details are passed along for a call back.
The company the caller is connected with diagnoses the problem, quotes its own price, does the work and
is paid directly by the customer. The line never charges the customer. The companies may pay the line
a referral fee (always disclose this).
Maurice's rule for this site (Oct 3 2026): no "we repair", no licensed or insured claims, and the
referral disclosure in every footer. The approved footer wording (Sep 25 2026): "[Brand] is a free
phone line, not a [trade contractor]. It answers your call and connects you with an independent local
[trade] company, which inspects, quotes and does the work, and which you pay directly."
Do NOT claim the connected companies are licensed or insured, even though Texas requires an ACR
license. Tell callers to ask for the TDLR license number and check it, and to ask for insurance.
Never name a partner company. Never say "our technicians", "our trucks", "we repair", "we install",
"we are licensed" or anything implying the line does the work.
