AC Repair for Garland

Garland is one of the most established cities in the DFW metroplex — 240,000 residents with housing that stretches from the 1950s ranch homes along Garland Road and Buckingham to the newer construction in Firewheel and northeast Garland near Rowlett. The older neighborhoods have systems that were designed for a different era of Texas summers, running on ductwork that has never been replaced.

What Garland Homeowners Call About

Aging systems in the established west Garland neighborhoods that need frequent repairs — capacitors, contactors, fan motors. At some point the repairs cost more than they are worth. We track the repair history and tell you when you have crossed that line.

Ductwork issues in the 1960s and 1970s homes — the original flex duct in these attics has often collapsed, torn, or separated from the plenum. The system runs perfectly but the air never reaches the rooms. We inspect the duct system as part of every diagnostic, not just the equipment.
