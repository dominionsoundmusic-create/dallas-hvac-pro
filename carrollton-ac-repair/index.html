AC Service for Carrollton

Carrollton sits at the junction of three counties — Dallas, Denton, and Collin — with 140,000 residents and a housing stock that spans from the 1970s ranch homes in south Carrollton near Trinity Mills to the newer builds in the Castle Hills and Hebron Parkway corridors. The Old Downtown Carrollton area near the DART station has a mix of restored historic homes and newer infill construction, each with different HVAC needs.

Carrollton's central location also means it shares weather patterns with both the Dallas heat island and the slightly cooler air from the Denton County open land to the north. Systems in south Carrollton near Addison and Farmers Branch run harder than systems in north Carrollton near The Colony — sometimes by several hundred hours per summer.
