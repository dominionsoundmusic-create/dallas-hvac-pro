What McKinney Homeowners Call About

AC stopped blowing cold air — the most common call. Usually a refrigerant issue, a failed capacitor, or a frozen evaporator coil. We diagnose it on site and quote the repair before any work starts.

System running but not keeping up — common in July and August when outdoor temperatures exceed 105. Sometimes the system is undersized for the home. Sometimes the ductwork is leaking conditioned air into the attic. We measure the temperature drop across the system and the static pressure in the ducts to find the real problem, not just the symptom.

New system installation — when repair costs approach half the price of replacement, or when R-410A recharges become uneconomical, it is time for a new system. We size replacements using Manual J load calculations matched to your home's actual square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork — not just the tonnage the old unit happened to be.
